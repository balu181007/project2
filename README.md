CostSaver Global Logistics OS is a comprehensive single-file logistics and supply chain management platform designed to optimize transportation, inventory, warehousing, and fleet operations across global networks. The platform integrates intelligent route planning, shipment tracking, fleet monitoring, warehouse management, inventory control, fuel cost analysis, delivery scheduling, and operational analytics into a unified system.

Built with interactive real-world mapping capabilities, CostSaver enables organizations to visualize transportation routes, compare logistics strategies, and identify the most cost-effective shipping options based on distance, urgency, fuel consumption, cargo type, traffic conditions, safety requirements, and delivery constraints. The platform supports multimodal transportation, including trucks, vans, containers, rail, air cargo, maritime shipping, and vehicle transport.

Users can manage warehouses, monitor stock levels, track inventory movement from source to destination, oversee in-transit shipments, and analyze logistics performance through dashboards and visual reports. Advanced cost calculation tools provide detailed insights into transportation expenses, fuel usage, operational efficiency, warehouse utilization, and supply chain bottlenecks.

Designed for logistics companies, manufacturers, distributors, retailers, freight operators, and supply chain professionals, CostSaver Global Logistics OS helps reduce transportation costs, improve delivery reliability, increase asset utilization, and enhance decision-making through data-driven logistics planning. Its single-file architecture ensures portability, simplified deployment, and accessibility while delivering enterprise-grade logistics management capabilities within a streamlined and user-friendly interface.
